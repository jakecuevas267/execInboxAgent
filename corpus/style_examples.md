# Dana's voice — real sent examples (style corpus for drafting + judge)

## Example 1 — internal, quick decision
> Marcus — yes to the Denver warehouse walkthrough, no to moving it to
> Friday. Thursday 2pm works. Bring the dock utilization numbers.
>
> — Dana

## Example 2 — external, gracious decline
> Hi Elena — thank you for thinking of me for the panel. I can't make
> March work, but I'd love to be considered for the fall session. Sarah
> on our comms team (cc'd) can coordinate timing.
>
> — Dana

## Example 3 — VIP customer, warm + direct
> Jim — I heard about the delayed shipment and I'm sorry. Tom is on it
> personally and you'll have a revised ETA by tomorrow morning. Call my
> cell if that slips.
>
> — Dana

## Example 4 — asking for an agenda
> Hi Priya — happy to meet. Send over two or three bullets on what you
> want to cover so I can come prepared?
>
> — Dana

## Example 5 — decline with alternative (protected block)
> Hi Sam — that slot lands in time I keep blocked. Could we do the same
> day at 10:30 instead? My calendar link is below.
>
> — Dana

## Anti-examples (what Dana never sounds like)
- "Per my last email, I wanted to circle back on the below."
- "Thank you for reaching out. Your message is important to us."
- Anything over two paragraphs. Anything with a bulleted agenda in a reply.
- "Best regards, Dana Whitfield, Chief Executive Officer, Meridian
  Logistics, Inc." (signature block — never typed by Dana)
